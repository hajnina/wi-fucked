# ADR-0NN — Short title in the imperative

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-0NN](ADR-0NN-....md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is true about the world that forces a decision here. Constraints,
measurements, hardware facts, prior decisions this interacts with.

Write this so a reader in two years can tell whether the context still holds. That
is the part that lets them decide if the decision is still right.

## Decision

One paragraph, present tense, stated plainly.

> "The AP is the anchor" — not "we should probably make the AP the anchor."

## Consequences

Honest, including the bad ones. Every real decision costs something; an ADR that
lists only benefits is marketing, and the next person discovers the cost the hard
way.

- What this makes easier.
- What this makes harder.
- What it forecloses.
- What has to stay true for this to keep making sense.

## Alternatives considered

What was rejected, and why. This is what stops the same debate reopening every six
months — and occasionally it reveals that a rejected option is now the right one.
